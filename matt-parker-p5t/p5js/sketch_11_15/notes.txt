What the port does:

sketch.js mirrors the Processing logic exactly:

- Two colors tracked: o (old) and p (current), stored as pre-computed RGB triplets

- When m < 1 || m >= n*n (no matching pixels, or canvas fully claimed): promote
p→o, generate a new random fully-saturated HSB color as p, seed it at (9,9) 

- Each frame: iterate all pixels, spread any pixel matching o or p to a random neighbor
(offset -2 to +1), count matches into m 

- loadPixels()/updatePixels() used instead
of get()/set() for performance Color mode: colorMode(HSB, n) →
color(random(480), 480, 480) gives fully saturated/bright colors at random hue,
matching color(random(n), n, n) from the original
